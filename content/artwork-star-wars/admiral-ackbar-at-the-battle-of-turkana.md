description: Admiral Ackbar at the Battle of Turkana. One of my favorite characters and my favorite capital ships. Ackbar
  rose from lowly slave of Grand Moff Tarkin to Supreme Commander of the New Republic Fleet. Without the noble Mon Calamari,
  it is unlikely the Alliance could have won the war. Supremely talented, direct, but yet possessing a sense of humor, Gial
  Ackbar led the Alliance Fleet and then New Republic Fleet against the greatest of the Imperial foes. From Captain Lennox,
  who he defeated 1 BBY from his and Mon Mothma’s flagship the Independence, to Grand Admiral Thrawn, to devising the final
  decisive fleet battle against the Yuzzhan Vong, Ackbar was a critically important leader. He was also very much a father
  figure to Princess Leia, who despised her own father for obvious reasons. He took a shine to her youngest son Anakin. Though
  he did not have any of his own children, he was very proud of his pilot niece Jesmin, and other niece Cilghal who was a
  Jedi healer.
