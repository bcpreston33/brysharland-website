description: 'Cindel, to me, is the bravest most incredible hero of Star Wars. Her intensely traumatic experiences on Endor
  left me wondering what happened to her after Noa saved her and they left together. Did you know there were plans for a third
  Ewok movie, but sadly it was cancelled. To my great joy Cindel appeared in the EU much later in adulthood as a New Republic
  journalist. She attended the academy for journalism on Coruscant and is pictured here 16 ABY upon her graduation. Her very
  proud adoptive papa, Noa Briqualon (Wilford Brimley), best friend in the universe Wicket Wystri Warrick, and old friend
  Teek are pictured here cheering her on. It was probably a very bittersweet day for her, thinking of how Catarine, Jeremitt,
  and Mace Towani were all gone. The tenacity of Cindel to give back to the galaxy after such personal tragedy is such an
  inspiring story. Hey if anyone at Lucasfilm is reading this I could make a really sweet comic about Cindel 😏 if you can
  make it through Ewoks: The Battle for Endor without crying you have no soul. This picture is dedicated to Aubree Miller,
  the wonderful child actress that played Cindel.'
