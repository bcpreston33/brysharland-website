description: For me Star Wars is much more about the untold stories that were more fleshed out in the expanded universe. But
  even there some things have always been left to the imagination however. I wanted to illustrate something I felt worth seeing;
  what happened to the very brother of Obi-Wan Kenobi, Owen Lars, and his wife Beru when finally confronted by the empire?
  Surely Owen, who in the comics managed to land the killing blow on Darth Maul with his blaster rifle, saving his estranged
  brother Obi-Wan would not go down without a fight? And surely a tough daughter of Tatooine like Beru Whitesun would not
  lose the opportunity to blast some fascist stormtroopers. And with their family in grave danger, would not their brave droids
  rush to protect them even going to their certain doom? Yes, the answer to all of these questions is yes, and that stormtrooper
  squad lost most of their men before the force sensitive couple of Owen and Beru fell in battle.
